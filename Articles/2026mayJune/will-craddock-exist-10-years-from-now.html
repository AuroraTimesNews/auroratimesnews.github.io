Will Craddock exist 10 years from now? May 25th 2026

By Zaydan DeMoss

Is Craddock gonna exist 10 years from now? Maybe, it depends on if the district goes on with their “Essential Plan”, but most likely they will build the new school over the 100 year old building.

For starters, why are they gonna get rid of Craddock? The Aurora City School District wants to get rid of Craddock so they can build new infrastructure that doesn’t have the problems that Craddock has, for example, some classrooms in the school get leaks at the windows when it rains which they have tried to fix. In addition, when I went to Craddock back during Covid, my second grade classroom had an issue with the power going out.

Second, what are they gonna build there? Craddock also accommodates the bus garage which they are also gonna replace. After they spend around $2,000,000 total in demolition of the bus garage and Craddock they are gonna spend another aprox. $64,000,000 on a new elementary school and bus garage. Craddock is a grades 1-2 school, the new school will accommodate grades 1-3 which will give Leighton elementary (grades 3-5) more space for grades 4-5.

In conclusion, is Craddock gonna exist 10 years from now? Probably not due to the district's need for new infrastructure, unless they get a new plan or can’t get the funds Craddock is most likely gonna be eliminated.
